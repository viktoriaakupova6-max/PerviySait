# Grammar: tenses

Present Simple: I study at college. He studies at college. Do you study here? — употребляется для регулярных действий и фактов. Present Continuous: I am studying now. — действие происходит в момент речи. Present Perfect: I have already finished the task. — результат к настоящему моменту.

Past Simple: We visited the library yesterday. Past Continuous: They were reading at 5 o'clock. Past Perfect: She had finished the report before the meeting started. Future Simple: I will help you. Be going to: We are going to visit the museum.

Signal words: always, usually, every day — Present Simple; now, at the moment, look! — Present Continuous; already, just, yet, ever, never, since, for — Present Perfect; yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago — Past Simple.

# Grammar: passive voice, conditionals, modals

Passive voice: The book was written by Pushkin. The letter will be sent tomorrow. Present Simple Passive: The library is cleaned every day. Форма образуется глаголом to be в нужном времени плюс третья форма смыслового глагола.

Conditionals: If it rains, we will stay at home (First Conditional — реальное условие). If I had more time, I would read more (Second Conditional — нереальное условие в настоящем). If I had known, I would have come (Third Conditional — нереальное условие в прошлом).

Modal verbs: can (ability), could (past ability, polite request), must (obligation), have to (external obligation), should (advice), may (permission, possibility), might (possibility). Примеры: You must wear safety glasses. You should revise the rules before the test. May I come in?

# Vocabulary: college, professions, technology

College life: timetable, lecture, seminar, workshop, laboratory, exam, term, mark, certificate, scholarship, library card, reading room. Professions: engineer, technician, mechanic, welder, designer, programmer, electrician, toolmaker.

Technology: machine tool, lathe, milling machine, drill, conveyor, engine, spare part, blueprint, measurement, tolerance, safety equipment, workshop overalls. Verbs: to design, to assemble, to repair, to measure, to adjust, to operate, to maintain.

Useful phrases for the classroom: Could you explain this again, please? What does "tolerance" mean? How do you spell it? I'm not sure, let me check. In my opinion, the best way is to…

# Reading: a short text about Ufa

Ufa is the capital of the Republic of Bashkortostan. It is a large industrial and cultural centre with a population of more than one million people. The city stands on the Belaya River, at the confluence with the Ufa River. Ufa is famous for its machine-building and oil-refining industries.

Our college, the Ufa Machine-Building College, trains specialists for local plants. Students study technology of machine building, engineering graphics, metrology and programming of CNC machines. The college library is on the ground floor of the administrative building at 32 Borisoglebskaya Street.

In the library, students can borrow books for one week. The reading room is open from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. from Monday to Friday. There is a lunch break from 12 to 1 p.m. On Saturday and Sunday the library is closed.

# Writing practice

A formal letter of request: Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to ask about… I would be grateful if you could send me information about… I look forward to hearing from you. Yours faithfully, Ivan Petrov.

An e-mail to a teacher: Subject: Question about the report. Dear Anna Ivanovna, Could you please tell me when the report is due? Thank you in advance. Best regards, Ivan.

A short report about a machine: The lathe is used for turning metal parts. It consists of a bed, a headstock, a tailstock and a carriage. The workpiece is fixed in the chuck and rotated, while the cutting tool moves along the surface. Safety rules must be followed: wear goggles and do not touch rotating parts.
